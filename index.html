Payment rules & escalation

FedEx vs. DHL payment rules
FedEx — tax > 13,000 PLN: pay the Proforma Invoice before release. Tax < 13,000 PLN: goods deliver first, FedEx invoices taxes/duties/fees later.

DHL — always pre-pay via their online debit-card link before delivery. ⚠️ Double-check the delivery address spelling on the payment page — historically almost always misspelled.
🚨 Non-door-to-door escalation (worst case)
1. Hire an independent customs agent to represent the company before the Customs Office.
2. Pay taxes manually from the business bank account, using the dedicated TAX transfer category.
3. Wait for the Customs Office to formally clear the package.
4. Travel to Wirażowa St. for physical pickup.
5. Pay terminal/holding charges on-site to LS Airport Services (~1,200 PLN).
6. Settle the agent's separate invoice — typically higher than standard FedEx/DHL clearance rates.
